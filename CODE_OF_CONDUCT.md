# Code of conduct

GuiKit contributors are expected to keep project spaces welcoming,
constructive, and focused on the work.

- Be respectful when discussing design and implementation choices.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for contributors with different backgrounds and experience.
- Do not use harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or unwanted
  personal attention.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these
expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner.
